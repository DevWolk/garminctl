Workouts

Describe a structured workout; it is saved in Garmin Connect and delivered to the watch over USB, or written to the watch directly without Connect.

New workout

Steps end by duration ("10:00", "90s"), distance ("400m", "2km"), calories or the lap button. Targets: "hr 140-160", "hr zone 3", "pace 4:30-4:45" (min/km), "speed 10-12" (km/h), "cadence 170-180", "power 200-250". A repeat group is {"repeat": 4, "steps": [...]}.

Garmin Connect

Sign in once. The watch then syncs with Garmin's cloud over USB the way Garmin Express did.

Sign in to Garmin

The password goes to Garmin's sign-in service only. What is kept is a session token in ~/.config/garminctl, as the phone app keeps one.

Sync

Uploads new activities and health files, then writes to the watch whatever Connect has queued: settings changes, Connect IQ app installs and updates, workouts, courses. Nothing is deleted from the watch.
